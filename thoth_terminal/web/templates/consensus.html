Model Rules

AnalystsUse as a Consensus lens by subject, firm, or person.
InternalShow as comparison, not part of sell-side consensus math.
Company SourcesRoute official claims to company/product facts, not price targets.
Directional NewsStore dated upside/downside events with expiry and analyst-refresh status.
Qualitative GuidanceUse prior value plus directional marker until an official number exists.
UndersupplyUse catalyst/event context unless shortage size, utilization, or price impact is quantified.
Contract priceKeep separate from spot price.
Market shareChart by default when shares close; keep the table beside it.
TAM / SAM / SOMTrack period, unit, source, and scope before aggregation.
Bad writesLocal agent cannot delete; steward cleanup needs review.
AliasesResolve existing vocabulary before adding a new metric or material name.
