One workspace

From a source to a considered view.

01 / Collect

Bring your material in.

Read documents, follow feeds, and work with datasets. Search across the material instead of rebuilding the same context each time.

02 / Investigate

Develop the argument.

Connect observations to companies, themes, and supply chains. Keep notes, theses, and unanswered questions alongside the evidence.

03 / Continue

Give agents useful context.

Desktop, web, and MCP tools share the same research objects. Agent work can contribute to the workspace you already use.

Your installation, your research

Work locally.
Choose what you connect.

Your installation stores research in your own PostgreSQL database and object store. You choose the sources, model providers, and integrations.

Connecting an external service sends it the information needed for that task. Reports become public only when you publish them.
